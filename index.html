About This Tool

The Direct Airways Fuel Planner is an internal dispatch planning tool used to evaluate multi-leg charter trips and determine the most economical fueling strategy.

The planner calculates leg distances, estimated fuel burn, remaining fuel onboard, and evaluates fuel waiver economics to determine where fuel should be purchased.

How To Use

  • Select the aircraft type.
  • Enter the estimated fuel onboard at the start of the trip.
  • Enter the route using comma-separated ICAO airport codes.
  • Optionally enter the next day's first leg.
  • Enter airport fuel information in the table.
  • Click Generate Fuel Plan.

Airport Data Fields

  • ICAO – Airport identifier
  • FBO – Service provider
  • Fuel Provider – Contract fuel provider
  • Fuel Price – Price per gallon
  • Handling Fee – Ramp or handling fee
  • Waive Gallons – Fuel required to waive fee

Unknown Data

If any information is unavailable, enter N/A in that field. The planner will treat N/A values as unknown and apply best judgement when making fueling decisions.

  • Unknown fuel price → Pay Fee, No Uplift
  • Unknown waiver or fee → Minimum Operational Uplift
  • KDAB → Always Uplift Max Fuel (contract fuel)

Planning Logic

  • Great-circle distance calculation
  • Aircraft-specific cruise speed and fuel burn
  • Remaining fuel tracking
  • Handling fee waiver economics
  • Effective fuel price calculation
  • Cheapest tanker detection
