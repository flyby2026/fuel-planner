About This Tool

The Direct Airways Fuel Planner evaluates multi-leg charter trips and determines the most economical fueling strategy using fuel prices, handling fees, and fee-waiver requirements.

The planner calculates great-circle distances, estimated fuel burn, remaining fuel onboard, and compares effective fuel pricing when waiving handling fees.

How To Use

  • Select the aircraft type.
  • Enter estimated fuel onboard at the start of the trip.
  • Enter the route using comma-separated ICAO airport codes.
  • Optionally enter the first leg of the next day.
  • Fill in airport fuel information.
  • Click Generate Fuel Plan.

Airport Data Fields

  • ICAO – Airport identifier
  • FBO – Service provider
  • Fuel Provider – Contract fuel provider
  • Fuel Price – Cost per gallon
  • Handling Fee – Ramp or handling fee
  • Waive Gallons – Minimum fuel required to waive the fee

Data Entry Standard – N/A Rule

If airport data is unknown, enter N/A instead of leaving the field blank. This allows the planner to continue evaluating the trip and apply best-judgment logic.

  • Fuel Price = N/A
    Fuel economics cannot be evaluated → Pay Fee, No Uplift
  • Handling Fee = N/A
    Fee comparison unavailable → Minimum Operational Uplift
  • Waive Gallons = N/A
    Fee waiver cannot be calculated → Minimum Operational Uplift
  • KDAB
    Contract fuel location → Always Uplift Max Fuel

These rules ensure the planner can still produce a recommendation even when complete pricing or fee information is unavailable.

Planning Logic

  • Great-circle distance calculation
  • Aircraft-specific cruise speed and fuel burn
  • Remaining fuel tracking
  • Handling fee waiver economics
  • Effective fuel price comparison
  • Cheapest tanker detection
