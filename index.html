About This Tool

The Direct Airways Fuel Planner is an internal dispatch tool designed to assist with planning multi-leg charter flights. The tool calculates leg distances, estimates fuel burn based on aircraft type, and evaluates fuel price and handling fee waiver economics to recommend the most cost-effective fueling strategy.

Results are displayed in an operations-ready format that can be easily copied into flight planning notes, dispatch briefings, or crew communications.

How To Use

1. Select Aircraft
Choose the aircraft type for the flight. Cruise speed and fuel burn estimates will adjust automatically.

2. Enter Starting Fuel
Enter the estimated fuel onboard at the start of the trip in gallons.

3. Enter Route
Input the flight route as a comma-separated list of ICAO airport codes.
Example:
KDAB,KGMU,KPWK,KEYE

4. Enter Next Day Leg (Optional)
If planning overnight stops, enter the next day's first leg to ensure enough fuel remains for departure.

5. Enter Airport Data

  • ICAO – Airport identifier
  • FBO – Service provider
  • Provider – Fuel contract provider
  • Fuel Price – Cost per gallon
  • Fee – Handling or landing fee
  • Waive – Gallons required to waive the fee

6. Generate Fuel Plan
Click the Generate button to calculate distances, burn estimates, and fuel purchase recommendations.

Planning Methodology

  • Great-circle distance calculation
  • Aircraft-specific cruise speed and burn rate
  • Remaining fuel tracking
  • Fuel price comparison between airports
  • Fee vs waiver cost analysis

Operational considerations such as payload, weather, alternates, and regulatory reserves should always be reviewed before finalizing fueling decisions.
