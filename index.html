About This Tool

The Direct Airways Fuel Planner is an internal dispatch tool designed to help quickly evaluate multi-leg flight routing and determine the most cost-effective fuel strategy. The planner estimates leg distances, calculates approximate fuel burn based on aircraft type, and compares fuel pricing and fee waivers between airports to suggest fueling decisions.

The output is formatted in an ops-ready style so the recommendation can be quickly copied into flight planning notes or dispatch communications.

How To Use

1. Select Aircraft
Choose the aircraft type for the trip. The planner adjusts cruise speed and fuel burn estimates accordingly.

2. Enter Route
Input the route as a comma-separated list of ICAO airport codes.
Example:
KDAB,KGMU,KPWK,KEYE,KGMU,KDAB

3. Enter Airport Data

  • ICAO – Airport identifier
  • FBO – Service provider
  • Fuel Price – Cost per gallon
  • Handling Fee – Landing or handling charge
  • Waive Gal – Minimum fuel uplift required to waive the fee

4. Generate Fuel Plan
Click the Generate button to calculate leg distances and produce a suggested fueling strategy.

Planning Methodology

  • Great-circle distance calculation between airports
  • Aircraft-specific cruise speed and fuel burn
  • Fuel price comparison between stops
  • Handling fee vs waiver uplift cost analysis

Based on these factors the planner recommends one of the following actions:

  • Uplift Max Fuel – Cheapest fuel location on route
  • Min Uplift to Waive Fee – Fuel purchase cheaper than paying fee
  • Pay Fee, No Uplift – Fuel purchase not economically justified

Results should always be reviewed with operational considerations including payload, weather, alternate requirements, and fuel reserve policies.
